What is Techorama?

Techorama is Netherlands leading conference for IT and Microsoft professionals. Join thousands of developers and architects for deep-dive sessions that go beyond the basics.

Expect practical content on .NET, Azure, AI, architecture, DevOps, web, and data.

This year, Techorama takes place as the Medieval Edition, adding a unique atmosphere to a deeply technical conference.
No fluff. Just real-world knowledge you can use right away.
